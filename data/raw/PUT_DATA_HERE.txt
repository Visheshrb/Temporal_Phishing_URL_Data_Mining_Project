Raw dataset layout:

  data/raw/phishing.zip   # full phishing folder archive (unzip into data/raw/)
  data/raw/phishing/      # local unzipped CSVs (gitignored)
  data/raw/benign/        # benign / dual-class sources

Unzip example (PowerShell):
  Expand-Archive data\raw\phishing.zip -DestinationPath data\raw -Force

Uncompressed data/raw/phishing/*.csv are meant to stay local / gitignored.
